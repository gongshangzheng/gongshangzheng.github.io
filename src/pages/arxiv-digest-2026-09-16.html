Fraglingo: Molecular Design via Attachment-Aware Autoregressive Fragment Generation

2026-09-15T04:00:00autoregressive, cs.AI2609.13519

中文标题：Fraglingo：基于附件感知自回归片段生成的分子设计

作者：Thao Nguyen, Jeonghwan Kim, Zhenhailong Wang, Heng Ji

摘要：

Molecular design is most effective when generation mirrors the edits chemists actually make: extending a scaffold, replacing a substituent, or decorating a scaffold at a specified attachment site while optimizing molecular properties. Fragment-based molecular design naturally supports this workflow, yet existing approaches often separate fragment selection from attachment prediction, first choosing a fragment from a fixed vocabulary and then predicting how it should be connected. This decoupling restricts generation to a closed fragment vocabulary and treats attachment as a separate prediction problem. We introduce Fraglingo, an autoregressive fragment-based molecular generator that jointly models fragment identity and attachment in a continuous latent space. Fraglingo predicts an attachment-aware fragment embedding and retrieves the next fragment through latent-space nearest-neighbor search. To encode attachment context, we introduce a wildcard-anchored readout that represents the growing molecule from the perspective of its active attachment site, enabling the predicted embedding to capture both the molecular context and the required attachment. Because generation operates in a continuous embedding space rather than over fixed fragment identifiers, new fragments can be added to the inference-time vocabulary without retraining, provided their embeddings can be computed by the trained fragment encoder. This retrieval-based formulation provides a unified generation primitive for molecule generation, scaffold generation, scaffold decoration, and molecular optimization. On controlled property-conditional benchmarks, Fraglingo achieves stronger joint property control than comparably trained baselines while maintaining competitive validity, uniqueness, and novelty. Furthermore, Fraglingo generalizes to fragment libraries up to 4x larger than those used during training without retraining.

摘要中文：

当生成过程模拟化学家实际进行的编辑操作时，分子设计最为有效：延伸骨架、替换取代基，或在指定连接位点装饰骨架的同时优化分子属性。基于片段的分子设计自然地支持这一工作流程，但现有方法通常将片段选择与附件预测分离，先从固定词汇表中选择片段，再预测其连接方式。这种解耦将生成限制在封闭的片段词汇表中，并将附件预测视为独立问题。我们提出Fraglingo，一个自回归的基于片段的分子生成器，在连续潜空间中共同建模片段身份和附件。Fraglingo预测附件感知的片段嵌入，并通过潜空间最近邻搜索检索下一个片段。为编码附件上下文，我们引入通配符锚定读出技术，从活跃连接位点的视角表示不断增长的分子，使预测的嵌入能够同时捕获分子上下文和所需附件。由于生成在连续嵌入空间中操作而非在固定片段标识符上运行，因此只要新片段的嵌入能由训练好的片段编码器计算，即可在推理时将其添加到词汇表中而无需重新训练。这种基于检索的 formulation 为分子生成、骨架生成、骨架装饰和分子优化提供了统一的生成原语。在受控的属性条件基准测试中，Fraglingo实现了比同等训练的基线更强的联合属性控制，同时保持了具有竞争力的有效性、唯一性和新颖性。此外，Fraglingo能够泛化到比训练时使用的片段库大最多4倍的片段库，且无需重新训练。

T-LoopFormer: Token-Level Elastic-Depth Looped Transformers for Latent Reasoning With Dynamic Routing

2026-09-15T04:00:00autoregressive, cs.AI2609.15160

中文标题：T-LoopFormer：用于潜在推理的词元级弹性深度循环Transformer及其动态路由

作者：Mingqian Yu, Wenpeng Zhang, Peilin Zhao

摘要：

Looped Transformers have recently demonstrated strong performance in both reasoning and language tasks by reusing a shared set of parameters across multiple iterations, achieving parameter efficiency without sacrificing representational power. Besides, looped Transformers perform inference directly in the latent space (latent reasoning) to reduce the number of tokens consumed during inference, thereby achieving improved sample efficiency. However, these models typically apply a fixed recursion depth uniformly to every token, leading to suboptimal compute allocation and leaving significant efficiency gains on the table. In this work, we propose \textbf{dynamic token-choice routing} for looped transformers, enabling each token to adaptively determine its own number of loop iterations based on its hidden state. We use a dynamic router to decide whether a token should continue recursing or exit early, allowing simple tokens to bypass unnecessary computation while hard tokens receive deeper processing. To ensure that this adaptive mechanism does not compromise decoding efficiency, we further introduce recursion-wise KV caching, which maintains an independent key-value cache for each recursion loop. This design ensures that tokens at different depths only attend to their corresponding cached states, effectively eliminating redundant computations for exited tokens and enabling fast autoregressive decoding. Extensive experiments show that T-LoopFormer reaches the sota performance under the same parameters on PPL and 10 zero-shot reasoning tasks, even surpassing the base model at 24x FLOPs and our model could reach the lowest inference latency, which validate the effectiveness of token-choice router and recursion-wise KV cache. Code: https://github.com/YuMingQian1234/T-LoopFormer.

循环Transformer近期在推理和语言任务中展现出卓越性能，其通过在多次迭代中复用共享参数集，在保持表示能力的同时实现了高效的参数利用。此外，循环Transformer直接在潜在空间进行推理（潜在推理），以减少推理过程中消耗的词元数量，从而获得更高的样本效率。然而，这些模型通常对所有词元应用统一的固定递归深度，导致计算分配次优，存在显著的效率提升空间。本工作提出循环Transformer的动态词元选择路由机制，使每个词元能够根据其隐藏状态自适应地确定自身的循环迭代次数。我们采用动态路由器来决定词元是继续递归还是提前退出，从而允许简单词元绕过不必要的计算，而困难词元获得更深入的处理。为确保这种自适应机制不影响解码效率，我们进一步引入递归级KV缓存，为每个递归循环维护独立的键值缓存。该设计确保不同深度的词元仅能访问其对应的缓存状态，有效消除了已退出词元的冗余计算，并支持快速自回归解码。大量实验表明，T-LoopFormer在相同参数条件下于PPL和10项零样本推理任务上达到了最优性能，甚至在24倍FLOPs条件下超越了基础模型，且我们的模型能达到最低的推理延迟，验证了词元选择路由器和递归级KV缓存的有效性。代码：https://github.com/YuMingQian1234/T-LoopFormer。

Generalization Can Emerge in Tabular Foundation Models From a Single Table

2026-09-15T04:00:00autoregressive, cs.AI, cs.LG2511.09665

中文标题：表格基础模型的泛化能力可从单张表格中涌现

作者：Junwei Ma, Nour Shaheen, Alex Labach, Amine Mhedhbi, Frank Hutter, Anthony L. Caterini, Valentin Thomas

摘要：

Deep tabular modelling increasingly relies on in-context learning where, during inference, a model receives a set of $(x,y)$ pairs as context and predicts labels for new inputs without weight updates. We challenge the prevailing view that broad generalization here requires pre-training on large synthetic corpora (e.g., TabPFN priors) or a large collection of real data (e.g., TabDPT training datasets), discovering that a relatively small amount of data suffices for generalization. We find that simple self-supervised pre-training on just a \emph{single} real table can produce surprisingly strong transfer across heterogeneous benchmarks. By systematically pre-training and evaluating on many diverse datasets, we analyze what aspects of the data are most important for building a Tabular Foundation Model (TFM) generalizing across domains. We then connect this to the pre-training procedure shared by most TFMs and show that the number and quality of \emph{tasks} one can construct from a dataset is key to downstream performance.

摘要中文：

深度表格建模越来越依赖于上下文学习，在推理过程中，模型接收一组(x,y)对作为上下文，并在不进行权重更新的情况下预测新输入的标签。我们挑战了一种流行观点，即在此实现广泛泛化需要在大规模合成语料库（如TabPFN先验知识）或大规模真实数据（如TabDPT训练数据集）上进行预训练，而是发现相对较少的数据就足以实现泛化。我们发现，仅在单个真实表格上进行简单的自监督预训练就能产生跨异构基准的惊人强迁移能力。通过在多个不同数据集上系统地进行预训练和评估，我们分析了构建跨领域泛化的表格基础模型（TFM）时数据的哪些方面最为重要。随后，我们将此与大多数TFM共享的预训练程序联系起来，并表明从数据集中可构建的任务数量和质量是下游性能的关键。

Conflict-Predictive Variable Horizons in Multi-Drone Distributed Model Predictive Control

2026-09-15T04:00:00autoregressive, cs.AI, cs.RO, cs.SY, eess.SY2609.13270

中文标题：多无人机分布式模型预测控制中的冲突预测可变时域

作者：Linda M\"{u}mken, Michael Schwung, Stefan Lier, Andreas Schwung

摘要：

In distributed model predictive control for multi-drone collision avoidance, a fixed prediction horizon forces a compromise: a short horizon is inexpensive but reacts late to approaching neighbors, whereas a long one anticipates conflicts at a per-step cost that grows superlinearly with its length. We propose a conflict-predictive variable horizon that each drone sets locally, leaving the distributed model predictive control itself unchanged. From a short history of observed positions, a drone extrapolates the flight lines of its neighbors, tests each against its own using confidence funnels that narrow with prediction range, and obtains each time to conflict in closed form. The horizon is then the smallest admissible value whose planning window covers the farthest predicted conflict. It collapses to its minimum in clear airspace and grows only when a conflict lies ahead. Provided this minimum meets a single computable feasibility bound, we prove that recursive feasibility and asymptotic stability are preserved for every horizon the policy can select. These guarantees hold for a linear model, and a cascaded inner loop reduces each quadrotor's translational dynamics to a perturbed double integrator, so they carry over to the linearized quadrotor model and, as practical stability, to the full nonlinear one. In simulation on dense antipodal-swap benchmarks, the variable horizon reduces both per-step solver cost and total computation well below those of a long fixed horizon, and it maintains separation in every run, which a short fixed horizon of comparable per-step cost does not.

摘要中文：

在多无人机避障的分布式模型预测控制中，固定的预测时域迫使人们做出权衡：短时域计算代价低，但对邻近无人机的接近反应迟缓；长时域能够预测冲突，但每步计算成本随时域长度超线性增长。我们提出一种冲突预测可变时域，由各无人机本地设定，不改变分布式模型预测控制本身。通过对观测位置的历史短序列进行外推，无人机推断出邻近无人机的飞行轨迹，利用随预测距离收窄的置信漏斗将每条轨迹与自身轨迹进行冲突检测，并闭式求解冲突发生时间。时域被设定为覆盖最远预测冲突的最小允许值。在clear airspace中时域收缩至最小值，仅当前方存在冲突时才增长。我们证明，只要这一最小值满足单一可计算的可行性界，策略可选择的每个时域都能保持递归可行性和渐近稳定性。这些保证在线性模型下成立，且级联内环将每个四旋翼的平移动力学降阶为扰动双积分器，因此它们可推广至线性化四旋翼模型，并以实用稳定性扩展至完整非线性模型。在密集对跖交换基准的仿真中，可变时域将每步求解器成本和总计算量均降至远低于固定长时域的水平，且在每次运行中均保持分离，而计算代价相近的短固定时域则无法做到。

One Spectrum, Two Resources: Data-Memory Scaling in Autoregressive Prediction

2026-09-15T04:00:00autoregressive, cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.LG2609.13500

中文标题：一个谱，两种资源：自回归预测中的数据-内存扩展定律

作者：Chiwun Yang, Xiaoyu Li

摘要：

How much learned memory is needed to benefit from more data? We show that the two resources are governed by one predictive-energy spectrum in a positive-entropy autoregressive retrieval source. Each coordinate contributes its query probability times the squared radius of its unknown logit. Writing $\mu$ for the resulting energy spectrum, we prove the minimax law $\mathfrak R^*_{\rm value}(n,B)\asymp_R \Phi_\mu(n^{-1})+\Phi_\mu(\tau_B), \Phi_\mu(t)=\int\min\{x,t\}\,\mu(\mathrm dx),$ for $n$ prediction blocks and a learned state with at most $2^B$ values. Data set the resolution $1/n$; memory sets the level $\tau_B$ reached by optimal bit allocation. The complete curve also recovers the positive spectrum. Energy-dimension pairing is essential: two causal sources with identical block-energy and block-dimension marginals have different data and memory exponents. A masked query-key attention head learns the route and values, realizing the law with explicit routing, format, and arithmetic errors. Further results give exponent-adaptive allocation, finite-precision realization, and compute-precision laws under two-sided arithmetic assumptions. Experiments recover the data-memory collapse and coupling exponents, explain the routing and allocation mechanisms, and examine weight-only quantization across six pretrained-model scales.

摘要中文：

需要多少学习内存才能从更多数据中受益？研究表明，在正熵自回归检索源中，这两种资源受同一预测能量谱管辖。每个坐标贡献其查询概率乘以未知logit的平方半径。设μ为所得能量谱，我们证明了极小极大法则$\mathfrak R^*_{\rm value}(n,B)\asymp_R \Phi_\mu(n^{-1})+\Phi_\mu(\tau_B), \Phi_\mu(t)=\int\min\{x,t\}\,\mu(\mathrm dx)$，其中$n$为预测块数，学习状态最多有$2^B$个值。数据集设置分辨率$1/n$；内存设置最优比特分配达到的水平$\tau_B$。完整曲线还能恢复正谱。能量-维度配对至关重要：两个具有相同块能量和块维度边际的因果源具有不同的数据和内存指数。掩码查询-键注意力头学习路由和值，以显式路由、格式和算术误差实现了该法则。进一步的结果给出了指数自适应分配、有限精度实现，以及双侧算术假设下的计算精度法则。实验恢复了数据-内存塌缩和耦合指数，解释了路由和分配机制，并检验了六个预训练模型尺度下的仅权重量化。

2026-09-15T04:00:00autoregressive, cs.AI, cs.SD, diffusion, eess.AS2609.13909

中文标题：DiTAR+：用于鲁棒自回归扩散语音合成的双重优化框架

作者：Ziyu Zhang, Tianlun Zuo, Hanzhao Li, Haoyu Zhang, Lei Xie

摘要：

Continuous-latent Autoregressive Diffusion Transformer (AR-DiT) models have demonstrated immense potential in zero-shot speech generation. However, they still suffer from limited decoding stability when synthesizing long utterances or complex linguistic structures. This instability primarily stems from a restricted historical receptive field and an acoustic inertia dependency within the diffusion decoder, which causes the model to ignore semantic conditions. To address these challenges, we propose DiTAR+, a dual-optimization framework. First, we introduce Dilated Context Sampling to expand the macro-level historical receptive field without violating physical temporal continuity, thereby preventing cumulative error propagation. Second, we propose Hierarchical Acoustic Masking to prevent shallow layers from attending to acoustic pre-context, explicitly decoupling semantic alignment from acoustic detail reconstruction. Extensive experiments show that our framework effectively mitigates pronunciation errors and semantic hallucinations, enhances generation robustness on challenging sentences, and maintains exceptionally high speaker similarity throughout the entirety of long-form utterances. On the linguistically challenging ZH-Hard set, DiTAR+ reduces the word error rate from 12.478% to 9.893%, and on extended utterances of 25 to 35 seconds it improves speaker similarity from 0.741 to 0.759 while simultaneously lowering the word error rate from 2.778% to 2.173%, outperforming both discrete-token and pure flow-matching baselines.

摘要中文：

连续潜变量自回归扩散Transformer（AR-DiT）模型在零样本语音生成方面展现出巨大潜力。然而，在合成长语音或复杂语言结构时，它们仍然面临解码稳定性不足的问题。这种不稳定性主要源于受限的历史感受野和扩散解码器中的声学惯性依赖，导致模型忽略语义条件。为解决这些挑战，我们提出了DiTAR+框架。首先，我们引入膨胀上下文采样来扩展宏级别的历史感受野，同时不违反物理时间连续性，从而防止累积误差传播。其次，我们提出分层声学掩码以防止浅层关注声学前向上下文，明确解耦语义对齐与声学细节重建。大量实验表明，我们的框架有效减轻了发音错误和语义幻觉，增强了挑战性句子的生成鲁棒性，并在整个长语音段落中保持了极高的说话人相似度。在具有语言挑战性的ZH-Hard数据集上，DiTAR+将词错误率从12.478%降低至9.893%，在25至35秒的扩展语音上，将说话人相似度从0.741提升至0.759，同时将词错误率从2.778%降低至2.173%，优于离散令牌和纯流匹配基线模型。
